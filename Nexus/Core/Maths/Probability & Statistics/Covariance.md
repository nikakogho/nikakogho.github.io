Between two random variables X and Y.

Measures whether variables move in same or opposite direction.
![covariance.png](covariance.png)

For variables A and B, with element count n, covariance is
$$ Cov(A, B) = \frac {\sum_i (A_i-A_{mean})(B_i-B_{mean})}{n-1} $$

## Correlation

$$ Corr(A, B) = \frac {Cov(A, B)}{\sqrt {Var(A)Var(B)} } $$
