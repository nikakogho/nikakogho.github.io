Most significant philosopher around the theme of [[Transhumanism]].

- Now works at [[Macrostrategy Research Initiative]]
- Founder and former head of [[Future of Humanity Institute]]
- Co-founder and former head of [World Transhumanist Association](https://www.humanityplus.org/) (now known as Humanity+), co-founded with [[David Pearce]]
- Coined [[Existential Risk]] in his [2002 paper](https://nickbostrom.com/papers/existential-risks/)
- Popularized [[AI Safety]] concerns like
	- [[Orthogonality Thesis]]
	- [[Instrumental Convergence]]
- Author of
	- Anthropic Bias: Observation Selection Effects in Science and Philosophy (2002)
	- Superintelligence (2014) - book that made AI x risk popular. Talks about all ways humanity might achieve superintelligence ([[Embryo Selection]], [[Genetic Engineering]], [[Neurotech]], [[Whole Brain Emulation|mind upload]] or [[AI]]), why in the long run AI has the most potential, [[Cosmic Endowment]], the light cone, and existential risk from AI
	- Deep Utopia: Life and Meaning in a Solved World (2024)
- Co-edited
	- Global Catastrophic Risks (2008) with Milan Ćirković
	- Human Enhancement (2009) with Julian Savulescu

Significant papers:
- [Are You Living In A Computer Simulation? (2003)](https://simulation-argument.com/simulation.pdf) - the [[Simulation Argument]] that Bostrom is most broadly known for
- [Future of Human Evolution (2003)](https://nickbostrom.com/fut/evolution.pdf) - arguing that by default, competition will drive us to a world of non-sentient uploads where all value has been lost, and that preventing it may require establishing a singleton (world government or worldwide enforced consensus to not race past some point)
- [Astronomical Waste (2003)](https://nickbostrom.com/papers/astronomical-waste/) - this makes 2 points:
	- There can be vastly more lives in the universe living vastly happier lives than is currently the case. If we are alone in our light cone, then this is essentially our responsibility. This makes handling of x risk on Earth a big deal.
	- Due to expansion of space, there is a finite and shrinking light cone that we can reach and the more we delay space colonization, more of the universe slips forever out of our reach, at the pace of ~3 galaxies per year. So maybe hurry.
- [Whole Brain Emulation Roadmap (2008)](https://gwern.net/doc/ai/scaling/hardware/2008-sandberg-wholebrainemulationroadmap.pdf) written with [[Anders Sandberg]] as a roadmap to develop [[Whole Brain Emulation|mind upload]] technology.
- [Information Hazards (2011)](https://nickbostrom.com/information-hazards.pdf) - Bostrom coined this term
- [The Vulnerable World Hypothesis (2019)](https://nickbostrom.com/papers/vulnerable.pdf) - idea that some technological inventions may cause human extinction
- [Propositions Concerning Digital Minds and Society (2021)](https://nickbostrom.com/propositions.pdf) - model welfare and related concerns
- [Optimal Timing For Superintelligence (2026)](https://nickbostrom.com/optimal.pdf) - arguing that if we only care about currently living people (person-affecting view) and completely disregard future generations (opposite stance from [[Longtermism]]), then given the potential upside from ASI, we should build it even if risk of human extinction from it is extremely high like 97%
