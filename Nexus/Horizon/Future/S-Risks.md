Risks of astronomical scale of suffering. Related, but distinct from, [[X-Risks]].

- Studied by [[Center for Long-Term Risk]] and [[Center for Reducing Suffering]]
- [80K Hours post](https://80000hours.org/problem-profiles/s-risks/)

Presented well in 1969 short horror story ["I have no mouth and I must scream"](https://galacticjourney.org/stories/I_Have_No_Mouth_and_I_Must_Scream_-_Harlan_Ellison.pdf).

We might get this if, for example, [[AI Alignment|misaligned superintelligence]] it decides it wants to minimize our utility, either because it must follow through on some threat from earlier for some complex [[Decision Theory]] reason or because we accidentally caused [[Emergent Misalignment of Large Language Models|emergent misalignment]] / "flipped the sign on its utility function".

We might also get something similar to this if a stable totalitarian world government is established.
