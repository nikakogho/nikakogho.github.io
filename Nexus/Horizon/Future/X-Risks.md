Existential risks, like risk of human extinction, or nuclear war that nearly wipes us out.
Related, but distinct from, [[S-Risks]].

First introduced by [[Nick Bostrom]] in his 2001 paper ["Existential Risks"](https://nickbostrom.com/papers/existential-risks/existential-risks.pdf).

Bostrom qualifies the risks into bangs, crunches, shrieks and whimpers:
- Bang is if we go extinct fast (meteor strike, paperclip maximizer, nuclear war...)
- Crunch is if we survive but forever become unable to reach the [[Transhumanism|transhuman]] utopia (cause some war brings population down to low or we invent some cultural idea or technology that keeps regressing us or there is a global totalitarian regime blocking science)
- Shriek is if we get some kind of posthuman state but nowhere near as good as we could have gotten ([[Forethought]] is trying to avoid this)
- Whimper is if we gradually lose all value and everything (like if uploads over time due to competition edit away their emotions and consciousness and all things we value, as described in Bostrom's 2004 paper ["Future of Human Evolution"](https://nickbostrom.com/fut/evolution)

- Asteroid/comet
- Climate change
- Some physics disaster like [[False Vacuum Decay|false vacuum decay]]
