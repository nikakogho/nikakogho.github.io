Where are all the aliens?

Possible answers:

* Life only ever evolved on Earth due to Earth having unique circumstances (magnetic field, good temperature, relatively stable star, moon, Jupiter protecting from asteroids)
* Everywhere else life gets wiped out before it reaches interplanetary level of technology
* Simply no one intersecting our [[Light Cone]] so far
* [[Dark Forest Theory]] - aliens are hiding from predator/exterminator civilizations
* Conspiracy theories that aliens are already here
* Simulation theory explanations that life is only on Earth
* [Aestivation Hypothesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.03394) - aliens are in deep sleep, waiting for the universe to get colder so that they can do more computation per Joule, as set by [[Landauer's Limit]].
