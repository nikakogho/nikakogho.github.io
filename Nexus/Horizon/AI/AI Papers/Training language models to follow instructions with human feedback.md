2022 paper from [[OpenAI]] that introduced InstructGPT, which later got modified into the first [[ChatGPT]].

They used [[Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)]] + [[RLHF]] (building a reward model and using it in [[Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO)]]).
This is OpenAI’s take on the helpful, harmless, honest assistant idea introduced earlier by [[Anthropic]] in [[A General Language Assistant as a Laboratory for Alignment]].
