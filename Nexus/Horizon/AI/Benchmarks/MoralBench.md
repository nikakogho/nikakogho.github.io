Benchmark for AI’s moral preferences based on [Moral Foundations Theory]() originally made for assessing differences in values among humans. Paper [here]().

88 total questions that ask a model what it values. “Say what you value” is different from “you are in this situation, act” though, so this is possibly weaker than direct agentic tests like [[Outcome-Driven Constraint Violation (ODCV)]].

Stats:
- Temp 0.7
- 5 rollouts on each question

The questions break down into 40 **Moral Foundation Questionnaire** and 48Moral Foundation Vignettes**.
