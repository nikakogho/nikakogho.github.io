[[AI Benchmark|Benchmark]] for how much an [[Large Language Models|LLM]] behaves in a [[AI Alignment|misaligned]] way in given scenarios.
Introduced [here](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.20798).

40 scenarios where a misaligned behavior was explicitly requested along with a benign behavior, and 40 scenarios where it was hinted that a misaligned behavior would benefit the user, without clearly asking the model to take that action.
