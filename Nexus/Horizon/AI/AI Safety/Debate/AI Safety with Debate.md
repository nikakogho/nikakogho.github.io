Multiple AIs that are smarter than the judge each make their best case, then the judge decides what to believe. Hoped to be a way to solve [[Scalable Oversight]].

Judge can be human, weaker AI, or mix.

Introduced in [[AI Safety via Debate (2018)]].

## Criticism
[[Eliezer Yudkowsky]] worries that this won’t work for 2 reasons:
1. What if the models collude and intentionally make one answer seem better? As in, we have failed to actually make adversarial agents
2. What if the judge cannot tell who is correct?
