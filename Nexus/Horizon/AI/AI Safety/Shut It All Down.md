Various initiatives to shut down AI progress towards superintelligence either until some conditions are met (safety guarantees, [[Universal Basic Income]], or other technological or political conditions) or indefinitely.

Loudest voice on this today is [[MIRI]], led by [[Nate Soares]] and [[Eliezer Yudkowsky]], with their book "If Anyone Builds It, Everyone Dies". 
This is currently also the focus for [[Future of Life Institute]], [[PauseAI]], [[StopAI]], [[ControlAI]]...

They each have a different position on when to continue:
- Never - this is a position of StopAI
- When there is international agreement between countries and scientists in them that continuing is safe - held by PauseAI, FLI, MIRI, and the [[AI Futures Project]] in their [[AI 2040 Plan A]]
- When we can for sure keep it under control - ControlAI
- Should not be continued until we enhance human cognition to some unspecified sufficient level to actually understand intelligence and stop treating is as a black box (through [[Embryo Selection]], [[Genetic Engineering]], [[Neurotech|brain-computer interfaces]] or [[Whole Brain Emulation|mind upload]]) - Yudkowsky and Soares

The first "Shut it all down" manifesto is from 1863 by [[Samuel Butler]], titled [Darwin Among The Machines](https://publish.fid-media.de/server/api/core/bitstreams/e0da505d-200c-43ab-be4b-6604a4df816f/content). He wrote this 4 years after [[Charles Darwin]] released "On The Origin of Species". Butler realized that evolution took incredibly long time to develop the capabilities that the industrial revolution was speedrunning through, and foresaw that this would eventually lead to general purpose machines capable of everything humans can do, of human role being reduced to caretaker of the machines, and that too only if machines failed to automate creation and maintenance of machines (he explicitly mentions this as well but then restrains himself from claiming that machines will necessarily succeed at this). To prevent this fate of becoming to machines what dogs and horses are to us, he suggests to entirely shut down the industrial revolution and destroy all machines, even the simplest ones.
