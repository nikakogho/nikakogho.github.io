Introduced by [[Paul Christiano]] in a [2016 essay](https://ai-alignment.com/prosaic-ai-control-b959644d79c2).

Idea that we may manage to [[AI Alignment|align]] a [[Deep Learning]]-based [[AGI]] without really understanding what we're doing (as opposed to [[Agent Foundations]] to truly mathematically understand intelligence and decision-making, or ambitious [[Mechanistic Interpretability]] to sufficiently understand how our [[Artificial Neural Network|neural networks]] work).

Most of today's AI alignment research is prosaic alignment.
