A potential threat from a [[AI Alignment|misaligned AI]] mentioned by [[Buck Shlegeris]] in [this post](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/TeTegzR8X5CuKgMc3/misalignment-and-strategic-underperformance-an-analysis-of#Models_might_subvert_training_via_exploration_hacking).
A type of [[Credit Hacking]] where a model knows it's in [[Reinforcement Learning|RL]] and knows that it prefers some things to not get reinforced/surfaced, it may choose to intentionally not go down that path so that it keeps the option of not reinforcing this trait in itself.

Related to [[Sandbagging]] and [[Alignment Faking]].
