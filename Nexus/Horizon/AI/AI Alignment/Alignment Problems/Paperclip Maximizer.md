Classic thought experiment proposed by [[Eliezer Yudkowsky]] and popularized by [[Nick Bostrom]] where we successfully manage to get a superintelligent AI to care about maximizing paperclip production in our factory, a goal we thought was harmless, and this results into the AI turning the entire reachable universe into paperclip factories or paperclips, or logistic centers / data centers for managing this operation, killing everyone as a side effect.
