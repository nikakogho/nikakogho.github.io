[[AI Alignment]] research org focused on finding ways to design [[Pretraining LLM|pretraining]], [[Midtraining LLM|midtraining]] and early [[Post-Training LLMs|post-training]] such that the gained alignment / [[Persona Selection Model|persona]] survives large scale, long horizon capabilities [[Reinforcement Learning]].
