Cells designed with opposite [[Chirality|chirality]] (handedness) from their natural counterparts.

Back in 2014, [[George Church]] proposed using it to give us forever virus-free organisms (including meat, plants or even humans).

If done wrong enough, it may give us invasive bacteria that spreads and eats all organic life, or eats all plants and we die as side effect. Could happen by accident, hence why biologists[ called for a ban](https://purl.stanford.edu/cv716pj4036) on it in 2024. It could also be done intentionally, made as a [[Bioweapons|bioweapon]] to end all life.

Unleashing mirror life is one of the [[X-Risks|existential risks]].
