Japanese [[Bioweapons|biological]] and chemical weapons R&D team in WW2, led by [[Shirō Ishii]].

Known as textbook example of [[War Crimes]].
They experimented with bioweapons, transmittable diseases, surgery without anesthesia, deadly vivisections, organ extraction, draining people from blood, freezing people to death, freezing parts of people, etc. on Chinese prisoners in Manchuria, sometimes publishing their findings in peer reviewed journals.

They also developed ways to use [[Germ Bombs|germ bombs]] carrying [[Anthrax]], [[Bubonic Plague]], [[Cholera]], etc.

Spared by Americans and allowed to live in USA after WW2 in exchange for the immense valuable medical and biowarfare/[[Biosecurity|biosecurity]] knowledge accumulated.
