Bombs or other ways of using bacteria, viruses, [insects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entomological_warfare), etc. to damage/kill plants, animals or people, either by directly infecting them or by poisoning water (a [[War Crimes|war crime]]).
Development and production of bioweapons is banned by the 1972 [Biological Weapons Convention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biological_Weapons_Convention). However, [[Biosecurity|defensive research]] is allowed.

Presently existing:
- [[Anthrax]]
- [[Glanders]]
- [[Botulism Toxin]]

In future this could mean:
- Large disease bombs
- Engineered pandemics
- [[Mirror Life]]
