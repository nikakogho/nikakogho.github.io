First introduced by [[J Storrs Hall]] in NASA in a 1993 paper [Utility fog: a universal physical substance](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19940022864/downloads/19940022864.pdf).

A [[Nanotechnology|nanotech]] swarm of micrometer size (human cell size) robots called *foglets* that should be able to simulate almost any physical material/object.

Such fog could fill the air and essentially act to transform the world into creative mode.

As proposed in paper, it would have some limitations:
- Fog itself would not be edible, though it can be transformed into cooking tools
- Fog itself cannot form some materials like parachutes
- Fog itself cannot do [[Atomically Precise Manufacturing (APM)]], for foglets are too large
- High heat issues?

Hall proposes building utility fog out of aluminum oxide instead of the typically offered carbon.

Utility fog is used in sci-fi story *Accelerando*.
